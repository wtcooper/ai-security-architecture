# Review: Fine-tuning and model registry pipeline

Files reviewed: `data/reference/architectures/cloud-training-pipeline.yaml` and its paired guidance.

## Overall assessment

The build-pipeline framing is right, but the graph omits the two tiers its own title and prose make load-bearing: the evaluation gate and the model registry. It also lets the training job absorb scanning and sign its own output, defeating independent promotion.

## Preserve

- Keep external ingest on the left and retain the recorded layout deviation.
- Keep exact dataset versioning, reproducibility records, separated corpus access, and load-time signature verification expectations.
- Keep data poisoning, unauthorized data, base/dependency supply chain, training-environment compromise, and model theft as core risks.

### Candidates for the weekly run

All of these came to light before this window opened, so a daily run does not add them. Each
needs its primary source read.

- **Atlassian Rovo "RovoBlast"** (Infosecurity Magazine, 20 Nov): a crafted link seeds
  instructions into Rovo, whose browsing agent then posts company data to the public web.
  Atlassian has fixed it. Prompt injection; likely an enterprise assistant pattern.
- **Famous Chollima's LLM-made malicious packages aimed at coding agents** (ReversingLabs,
  20 Nov): npm, plus `scraper-npm` on PyPI. Supply chain.
- **Jailbroken AI agent helps steal customer data and 8.6 GB of European government files**
  (GBHackers, 23 Nov). AI as the attacker; find the primary.
- **AI coding assistant leaks production passwords and HR tokens across 10 sessions**
  (TechRadar, 22 Nov). Identity; find the primary and check that an attacker was involved.
- **VulnCheck canary data: 1,756 attempts on AI-provider credentials, and coding-agent
  credential files (`~/.claude/.credentials.json`, `~/.codex/auth.json`) requested**
  (vulncheck.com/blog/credential-harvesting, 23 Nov). Identity; could be an `alsoSeen` on
  llmjacking-gateway-keys.
- **CERT-In: AI-assisted attacks lead to unauthorised payments at banks and fintechs**
  (The Hacker News, 23 Nov). Probably a follow-up to crafty-falcon-bfsi; check for a new
  CERT-In advisory.
- **GreyNoise GT13 campaign against Check Point Security Management servers** (GreyNoise,
  23 Nov; SecurityWeek, 24 Nov): 22+ organisations and 24 servers. GreyNoise only *believes*
  the actor used LLMs and agentic harnesses and cites no direct evidence. Watch for
  corroboration before filing it as AI as the attacker.

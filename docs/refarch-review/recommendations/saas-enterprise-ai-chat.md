# Review: Enterprise AI chat with connectors

Files reviewed: `data/reference/architectures/saas-enterprise-ai-chat.yaml` and its paired guidance.

## Overall assessment

The tenant-versus-consumer distinction and upload blind spot are important. The file still describes the retired tenant-wide assistant as a separate neighbor, contains obsolete flow data, and contradicts itself about whether prompts/uploads can be inspected.

## Preserve

- Keep corporate-tenant and personal-account paths visibly separate.
- Keep file/paste upload distinct from connector authorization.
- Keep opaque vendor internals, customer-operated internal tool admission, and audit fusion after fixing control loci.

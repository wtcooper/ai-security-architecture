Files reviewed: `data/reference/architectures/endpoint-personal-agent.yaml` and its paired guidance.

## Overall assessment

The whole-harness sandbox and ambient-channel threat model are valuable. This file needs a focused cleanup: it contains an obsolete second flow grammar, contradicts its own containment claim, and places identity/injection controls where the organisation cannot enforce them.

## Preserve

- Keep the whole-harness sandbox boundary and explicit workspace opening.
- Keep unsolicited senders, durable memory, heartbeat execution, tool supply, and runaway loops as distinct risk mechanisms.
- Keep messaging relay, model/tool gateway, internal tools, external tools, and enterprise data separate after correcting ownership and supply paths.

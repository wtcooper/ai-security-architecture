> **Status (2026-09-01): APPLIED** on branch `refarch-review-updates`. Every finding was

Files reviewed: `data/reference/architectures/endpoint-coding-agent-first-party.yaml` and its paired guidance.

## Overall assessment

The custody distinction is valid, but several copied claims still describe the third-party sibling. More importantly, package delivery and remote admission are drawn through components that cannot perform the stated jobs.

## Preserve

- Keep this architecture separate from the vendor-supplied sibling; custody, software supply, and remote admission are graph-level differences.
- Keep the private registry as a component, but connect it through the real distribution path.
- Keep the exposed local API/relay scenario and the explicit target-versus-common-state distinction.

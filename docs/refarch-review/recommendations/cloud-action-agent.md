Files reviewed: `data/reference/architectures/cloud-action-agent.yaml` and its paired guidance.

## Overall assessment

The deterministic action-catalogue and reversibility framing are strong. The target-state claim is weakened by in-process tools that bypass the gateway but have no data paths, and by several management controls pinned on runtime traffic.

## Preserve

- Keep deterministic, parameter-validated actions and compensating transactions as the primary design rule.
- Keep case-scoped state, per-call authorization, workload identity, and separate internal/external tool services.
- Keep consequence-based approval rather than confidence-based approval.
